News tips

Email the community desk

Every submission is reviewed by the publisher before anything appears on the website. Include a reply email so questions can be verified.

Urgent information

Use the right emergency channel

Never rely on social media or this website alone for life-safety emergencies; call 911 when appropriate.

Helpful details

Make a tip easy to verify

  • What happened and where
  • The date and approximate time
  • A direct source or official link
  • Your preferred contact information
  • Permission details for any submitted photo or video
Corrections

Accuracy comes first

Include the story headline, the statement that needs review and a reliable supporting source. Material corrections should be noted transparently on the story.
