Website terms

A few things readers should know.

Tri-State News & Weather shares helpful local information, but conditions and plans can change quickly. Use good judgment and check official sources when safety is involved.

Good to knowThese terms should receive a final review as the website adds email, advertising or other services.

Informational purpose

News, forecasts, traffic reports, business updates and event details are provided for general information. Conditions and plans can change quickly; readers should verify critical information with the responsible official source.

Emergency information

The website is not an emergency alerting system. Readers should follow official National Weather Service products, local emergency management instructions, NOAA Weather Radio and 911 guidance.

Community submissions

People who submit tips, photos or video should own the material or have permission to share it. Submissions may be edited for clarity, length, safety and accuracy.

Advertising

Advertisements are clearly labeled. The presence of a Google advertisement does not constitute an editorial endorsement.

External services

The site may link to government, social, mapping, analytics, email and advertising services. Those services operate under their own terms and policies.

Changes and contact

These terms may change as the website adds new features. Questions can be emailed directly to Harrison.
